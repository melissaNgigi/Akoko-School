About Our Department

Welcome to the Languages Department at St. Paul's Akoko Secondary School. Our department is dedicated to developing students' communication skills in both English and Kiswahili. We emphasize practical language skills, literature appreciation, and effective communication.

Subjects Offered:

  • English: Language skills, literature, and composition
  • Kiswahili: Language proficiency, literature, and cultural studies
  • Literature in English: Study of prose, poetry, and drama
